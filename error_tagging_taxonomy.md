# LLM-Quality-Assurance-Taxonomy

## Overview
A comprehensive Quality Assurance (QA) framework and error-tagging taxonomy designed for evaluating generative AI outputs, prompt optimization, dataset cleansing, and multi-turn model interactions.

## Core Components
* **Error-Tagging Taxonomy:** Categorization schema for identifying syntactical errors, reasoning gaps, formatting failures, and instruction-following deficiencies.
* **Multi-Tier Evaluation Rubrics:** Scoring matrices (1 to 5 scale) measuring response precision, depth of reasoning, and logical consistency.
* **Dataset Quality Control Guidelines:** Step-by-step verification protocols to maintain high precision, consistency, and reproducibility across global annotation teams.

## Key QA Metrics & Focus Areas
1. **Instruction Adherence:** Ensuring outputs strictly follow formatting, length, constraints, and specific user prompts.
2. **Logical Coherence:** Evaluating multi-step reasoning processes for soundness and step-by-step accuracy.
3. **Multilingual Quality Control:** Verifying translation accuracy, cultural nuances, and linguistic alignment across English, Urdu, Arabic, Indonesian, Hindi, and Pashto datasets.
